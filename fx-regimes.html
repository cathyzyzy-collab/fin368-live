Foreign Exchange Reserves

Total official foreign exchange reserves including gold (USD billions). Large reserves let central banks intervene in currency markets to defend pegs or smooth excessive volatility. China, Japan, and Switzerland hold the largest stockpiles relative to GDP.

Exchange Rate Regime Classification

Regimes range from hard pegs (currency boards, dollarization) to free floats. Intermediate "managed float" regimes involve regular central bank intervention to smooth moves. Based on IMF Annual Report on Exchange Arrangements (AREAER); classifications updated annually and may lag policy changes by months.

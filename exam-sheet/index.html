FIN368 function reference
Rates must be decimals. Type 0.10, not 10%. Percent literals give #NAME?. For monthly rates from an annual figure: 0.05/12.

Capital budgeting

=NPV(rate, val1, val2, ...) Discounts each value starting at t = 1. To include the t = 0 outlay, add it separately.
Canonical: =NPV(0.10, A2:A5) + A1   (A1 is the outlay, signed negative)
=IRR(values, [guess]) Cash-flow series must include at least one negative and one positive.
Example: =IRR(A1:A5)
=MIRR(values, finance_rate, reinvest_rate) Both rates required, as decimals. Example: =MIRR(A1:A5, 0.06, 0.10)
=XNPV(rate, values, dates) NPV with non-uniform dates. Dates can be a range of date cells.
=XIRR(values, dates, [guess]) IRR with non-uniform dates.

Loans & time value

=PMT(rate, nper, pv, [fv], [type]) Payment per period. Mortgage: =PMT(0.05/12, 360, -200000)
=PV(rate, nper, pmt, [fv], [type]) Present value of an annuity / lump sum.
=FV(rate, nper, pmt, [pv], [type]) Future value.
=RATE(nper, pmt, pv, [fv], [type], [guess]) Solves for the periodic rate.
=NPER(rate, pmt, pv, [fv], [type]) Solves for number of periods.

Statistics & risk

=SUM(range)   =AVERAGE(range)Standard.
=STDEV(range)   =STDEVP(range) Sample / population standard deviation. The STDEV alias is installed for you.
=VAR(range)   =VARP(range) Sample / population variance. The VAR alias is installed.
=CORREL(rangeX, rangeY)Correlation coefficient.
=COVAR(rangeX, rangeY)Population covariance (COVAR alias installed).
=MIN, =MAX, =COUNT, =COUNTAStandard.

Math & logic

=POWER(base, exp) or ^ =POWER(1.05, 10)   or   =1.05^10
=SQRT, =EXP, =LN, =LOG, =LOG10Standard.
=ABS, =ROUND, =INTStandard.
=IF(cond, then, else)   =AND, =OR, =NOTLogical.
Need a value as a percent? Multiply by 100 yourself: =B5*100. Or right-click the cell → Format → Percent (display only, doesn't change the value).

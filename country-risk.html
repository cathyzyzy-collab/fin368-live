GDP per Capita

GDP per capita (current USD) is the broadest proxy for income level and economic development. Countries above ~$12,000 are classified as high-income by the World Bank. Higher per-capita income generally correlates with lower political and credit risk.

Inflation & External Balance

Inflation erodes purchasing power and signals monetary (in)stability. The current account (% of GDP) measures net flows of goods, services and transfers. Persistent deficits must be financed externally and can create currency vulnerability.

Data sources & update frequency
OECD Short-Term Economic Statistics (STES) — interest rates, bond yields — monthly, ~4–6 week lag
Frankfurter / ECB reference rates — FX spot and history — daily (business days)
World Bank Open Data — GDP, inflation, current account, FX reserves — annual, 1–2 year lag
IMF DataMapper — inflation forecasts — annual update (current-year estimate available)
The Economist Big Mac Index CSV — implied PPP, raw prices — updated January & July
IMF AREAER — regime classifications — annual publication, static in this module

Annualized Historical Volatility vs USD

Historical volatility = annualized standard deviation of daily log returns (×√252). A 10% annualized vol implies a typical daily move of ~0.63%. Higher vol = more FX risk for importers, exporters, and unhedged foreign-currency positions.
